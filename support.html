Frequently asked questions

What is Running Theory?

Running Theory is a story-mode running app for iPhone. Each run is a 20-minute audio episode of an adventure set in desert canyons full of buried cities. Dr. Joseph Kidusa guides you from base camp over your headphones while you act as his legs in the field.

Do I need to be a fast runner?

No. Pace challenges adjust to your own speed, so the story works whether you're jogging your first mile or training for a marathon.

How do I answer riddles and make choices?

Speak your answer out loud, or respond by changing your pace when the story asks you to. Wrong answers have consequences in the story, so choose carefully.

Can I pause in the middle of an episode?

Yes. You can pause and resume anytime, and the episode picks up right where you left off.

What is the secret word?

Every five-episode season hides a secret word. You piece it together from clues after your runs, and solving it unlocks what comes next.

How much does it cost?

The first season is free.

What do I need to play?

An iPhone and a pair of headphones. Voice answers use your headphone or iPhone microphone.

I found a bug or have an idea. Where do I send it?

Email hello@ventrovastudios.com. If you're reporting a problem, let us know your iPhone model, iOS version, and which episode you were on.
