Running Theory

A story-mode running app. Every run is a 20-minute audio episode set in desert canyons full of buried cities, and you're the one in the field.

  • Be his legs in the fieldDr. Joseph Kidusa, a disgraced professor stuck at base camp, guides you through every step over your headphones.
  • Choose with your voice or your feetAnswer riddles out loud or by changing your pace. Get it wrong and you may be outrunning a collapsing passage.
  • Crack the season's secret wordEach five-episode season hides a word you piece together after your runs to unlock what comes next.
  • Built for any runnerPace challenges adapt to your own speed, and you can pause and resume anytime.
